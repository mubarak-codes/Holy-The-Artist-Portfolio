About me

I'm Mubarak Owosheni Oluwatunmishe — Holy The Artist.
          I make art using letters, numbers and symbols 
My path into art wasn't planned. I came across a TikTok comment where someone had drawn a house using nothing but simple text characters and emoji. I'd never seen anything like it. I made research and found out I only few people are doing it.

That was the beginning. I make my art using text characters — some pieces built on my phone, others hand-drawn on paper — turning plain characters into a portrait, character by character.

Digital and traditional Ascii Art
